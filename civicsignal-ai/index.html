Civic Innovation

How CivicSignal AI Bridges the Gap

Transforming bureaucratic official circulars into actionable, easy-to-read knowledge for every citizen.

1

1. Complex Official Circular

Government announcements are typically released as dense 5-10 page PDF orders with heavy administrative, technical, and legal terminology.

2

2. Structured Civic Extraction

CivicSignal AI identifies what matters: Does this affect my ward? When does it happen? What practical steps must I take?

3

3. Bilingual Citizen Accessibility

Citizens view summaries in their preferred regional language (Telugu or English) on any mobile phone, with interactive task checklists.
